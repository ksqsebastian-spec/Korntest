About us

Mehlig stands for the finest window expertise in exclusive, international villa construction. The family business was founded in 1946 and specialises in exceptionally demanding projects for architects and clients with extraordinary expectations of design, advice, product and service quality across the world of architecture and window technology. Mehlig earned its leading position in the market through countless joint projects with internationally renowned architects, and as the inventor of innovative window systems such as the »Sprosse 22« – a beautiful, refined and highly functional window construction that saved many houses from being ruined by renovation.

The perfect interplay of
beauty and technology

How does a North German joinery become an international brand?

WM: We always wanted the exceptional: to create something unique together with architects and to find, for every project, the ideal balance of technology and beauty – that is what drives us. Exclusive villa construction is therefore a perfect fit for us.

What does »beauty« mean in this context?

WM: Windows were once status symbols and an expression of good taste. In the last century that was often forgotten – so Mehlig developed and patented the Sprosse 22: a beautiful, refined and highly functional window construction for renovations in keeping with heritage protection.

What can I expect from Mehlig as an architect and client?

WM: An unrivalled package from a single source – consulting, detailed planning, product, installation and service for exclusive villas anywhere in the world. As a client I receive windows and doors that stay beautiful, functional and valuable for generations.

Which architects has Mehlig worked with?

WM: To name a few: Robert Stern, Frank O. Gehry, David Chipperfield, Gerkan Marg und Partner, Francis Terry, Sebastian Treese, Ulrike Krages, Hans Kollhoff, Allan Shope, as well as Randall Stofft and John Cooney.
