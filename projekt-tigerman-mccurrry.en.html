Hurrican Approved

Architect: Tigerman McCurryLocation: Palm Beach, Florida (USA)

The Chicago architecture team Tigerman McCurry works as successfully on large public buildings as on the design of private residences. The Mediterranean-style private villa shown stands in Palm Beach, right on the Atlantic.

The Mehlig window elements with round-arch transoms are unusually tall. The visible hardware (handles, for example) comes from the traditional Belgian house Vervloet Faes. The concealed locking elements are made of stainless steel to prevent corrosion caused by sea air.

We delivered this project together with our American partner Tischler und Sohn (USA) Ltd.
