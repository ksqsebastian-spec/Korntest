Architect: The Office of Thierry W. DespontLocation: Palm Beach, Florida (USA)

The Frenchman Thierry W. Despont lives and works as an architect in New York. Among other things, he is responsible for the interior design of Bill Gates’ private house in Seattle and of the new “Getty Center” museum in the hills above Los Angeles.

For the private house shown, in Palm Beach, Florida, Despont also designed the windows and doors, developed and built by Mehlig. Two windows measuring 10 by 6.50 metres, facing the sea and a tropical park, posed particular challenges — fully retractable into the floor via specially developed hydraulics, and hurricane-tested.

We delivered this project together with our American partner Tischler und Sohn (USA) Ltd.
