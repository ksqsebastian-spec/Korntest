We’re hiring

Project Manager / Work Scheduler (m/f/d)

To strengthen our team, we’re looking for a Project Manager / Work Scheduler (m/f/d) for timber windows and doors.

The daily challenge is the professional planning and preparation of the work-scheduling documents for the production of our high-quality products. Technical sophistication driven by special climatic conditions or burglary-protection requirements, paired with exclusive expectations of workmanship and function, makes every project unique.

Your responsibilities include

  • Managing projects for our American sales partner
  • Preparing the work-scheduling documents
  • Order-related material procurement
  • Invoicing (interim and final invoices)

What we’re looking for

  • Several years’ experience as a wood engineer, wood technician, master joiner or woodworking mechanic
  • Organisational talent with a structured, independent way of working
  • A quick grasp of technical details
  • Very good AutoCAD skills
  • Good written and spoken English

What we offer

  • Independent work with room to shape it (remote work, flexible hours)
  • A collegial atmosphere with flat hierarchies
  • A secure job in a family-run company (31 days holiday)
  • Internal and external training opportunities
Sales Representative (m/f/d)

To strengthen our team, we’re looking for a Sales Representative (m/f/d) for timber windows and doors.

The daily challenge is professional consulting and nurturing relationships with our clients and partners. A team of internal and external advisors supports you. You are the face of our company.

Your responsibilities include

  • Implementing our sales strategy
  • Marketing the company’s products
  • New customer acquisition, retaining and nurturing existing clients
  • Supporting quotes and costing
  • Maintaining partner relationships (e.g. Silatec, AirLux)

What we’re looking for

  • Several years’ experience as a wood engineer, wood technician, master joiner in sales, or architect
  • A communicative, professional manner
  • Willingness to travel
  • Good English; additional languages a plus

What we offer

  • Structured, qualifying onboarding
  • Independent work with room to shape it
  • Performance-based pay (fixed salary plus commission, company phone)
  • Internal and external training opportunities
Joiner (m/f/d)

To strengthen our team, we’re looking for a Joiner (m/f/d).

Our internationally positioned family business has moved its production process to a modern team concept. Fabrication staff are grouped into teams and take an active part in planning and delivering the orders.

Your responsibilities include

  • Woodworking and wood finishing for window and door elements
  • Cutting raw timber for the production of windows, doors and add-on parts
  • Making glazing bars, glazing beads and accessories
  • Operating all common woodworking machines

What we’re looking for

  • Completed training as a joiner or cabinetmaker
  • Manual skill and technical understanding
  • High quality awareness and precise work
  • Basic CNC knowledge desirable

What we offer

  • Independent work with room to shape it
  • A collegial, honest working atmosphere
  • Performance-based pay with capital-forming benefits
  • A varied range of tasks
